It was populated more than 3000 years ago and is estimated to be more than 5.000.000 years old. It's a breathtaking natural monument. If you get the chance I recommend to book a helicopter flight. It's breathtaking.

Whenever I need a stronger HDR effect, I used to use ProHDR App. However, in 2020, the App was removed from the App Store. If you're looking for an HDR app or an App to take bracketed RAW photos, have a look at [ProCamera App](/blog/procamera-app-review) as an alternative. I've create a page, where I list all the [recommended iPhone Camera Apps](/iphone-photography#iphone-photography/camera-apps) that I rely on.

To bring out more details in the photo I used Intensify Pro App on my Mac. As the name suggests, Intensify Pro help you to intensify your photos in various aspects. The pro version even supports layers and brusher to apply effects only to a part of a photo.
